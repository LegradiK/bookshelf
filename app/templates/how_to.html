How to use your bookshelf

Everything you need to know, one step at a time.

Find a book or books to add to your shelf

  1. Click + Add a book on the right top corner.
  2. Type the name of a book, or the person who wrote it, into the search box.
  3. Wait a moment and the books will appear.
  4. Can't see your book? Press Load more at the bottom to see more.
  5. Then choose a reading status: Want to read, Reading now or Finished.
  6. Press Add under the book you want.

Want to add lots of books at once? Tick the box on each book, then press Add selected books at the bottom of the screen.

Choose a book that has a cover picture if you can. Books without a cover often have missing information, so things like finding genres may not work.

Your book page

Click on any book on your shelf to open its page. Here you can:

  • Change its status: choose from the menu under the cover. It saves straight away.
  • Change the genre: press the pencil, type the genres with commas between them, then press Save.
  • Find genres for you: press the magnifying glass. If new genres are found, you'll be asked if you want to add them. Press OK to add them or Cancel to leave things as they are.
  • Times read: once a book is marked as Finished, you can type how many times you've read it and press Save.

Log a reading and write reviews

  1. On the book page, press + Log a reading.
  2. Check the date. It's set to today, but you can change it.
  3. Choose how many stars to give the book, from 1 to 5.
  4. Write what you thought if you like. What was your favourite part?
  5. Press Save reading.

Your readings appear under Reading history. To delete one, press the bin next to it.

Earn certificates

Every book you finish counts towards your next reading certificate. Keep reading and see how many you can earn!

To see how close you are to your next certificate, go to the Achievements page. You can also download your newest certificate there.

Questions

I pressed Add but nothing happened.

Check that you've chosen a reading status first. Books can't be added without one.

How do I take a book off my shelf?

There are three ways:

  • From the book's page: press Remove from shelf at the bottom.
  • From your bookshelf: press the bin in the bottom right corner of the book.
  • Lots of books at once: tick the box on each book, then press Delete selected at the bottom of the screen.

Don't worry, you'll always be asked to check before anything is removed.

Can I read a book more than once?

Yes! Log a new reading each time. You can give it different stars and a new review.

The book has no cover picture.

Some books don't have a cover online, so you'll see the first letter of the title instead. These books often have other information missing too. When you search, try to pick a version of the book that has a cover.
